Support

Puff Daddy helps you track and quit nicotine — vapes, pouches, or cigarettes. If something isn’t working or you have a question, email us and we’ll get back to you.

Contact us

Email dulaj@pier94talent.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple account, not by us. To view, change, or cancel:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions
  • Select Puff Daddy and choose your option

Cancelling at least 24 hours before your renewal date stops the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Restore a previous purchase

Reinstalled the app or changed device? Open Puff Daddy and tap Restore on the subscription screen while signed in with the same Apple account you purchased with.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account and its data from inside the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile, logs, and group activity from our servers and cannot be undone.

A note on the numbers

Nicotine amounts, ingredients, costs, savings, and health figures shown in the app are estimates for tracking and motivation only. Puff Daddy is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about quitting.
